# EU Cloud Exit Readiness — competition and entry wedge (2026-09-17)

## Decision
KEEP, but narrow the first sale.

A generic "cloud exit readiness assessment" is already a product category. EscapeCloud markets a cloud-exit readiness platform with an open-source/community engine, SaaS scoring/reports, and enterprise self-hosting. That is positive market evidence, but it makes a questionnaire/scoring product a weak entry wedge.

Source: https://escapecloud.io/

The better initial wedge is **provider-side executable verification**: test whether a SaaS/cloud provider can actually honour one representative customer's exit request. This remains distinct from buyer-side vendor-lock-in scoring.

## Regulatory forcing function
Data Act Chapter VI switching duties are already live. Practitioner/legal sources consistently identify 12 January 2027 as the point when switching charges, including switching-related data-egress charges, must be removed. Standard service fees and proportionate early-termination penalties are distinct; parallel/multi-cloud egress has separate treatment. Do not market this as "all egress becomes free".

Primary legal source remains Regulation (EU) 2023/2854, especially Articles 25–31:
https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/2854/oj

Useful corroboration:

## Lower-friction entry product
Before asking for a test tenant, offer a **Cloud Exit Surface Audit** using only public/provider-supplied non-secret material:

1. public terms / order-form template;
2. switching and termination wording;
3. public pricing and any exit/egress/support charges;
4. help-centre export instructions;
5. API/export documentation;
6. public description/register of data structures and formats where applicable;
7. stated retention/deletion process.

Output: a one-page contradiction map with `OBSERVED / NOT FOUND / NEEDS INTERNAL TEST` and 3–5 highest-risk questions.

This is deliberately not the paid end product. Its job is to create evidence and make the deeper **Cloud Exit Drill** easier to sell. Never infer compliance from absence of public evidence.

## Funnel hypothesis
`public surface audit -> contradiction found -> provider conversation -> test-tenant exit drill -> remediation/retest -> partner repeatability`

This gives Wealth Engine something it can partially execute autonomously from public evidence while keeping the decisive commercial gate human/buyer-based.

## Pricing update
Do not lower the existing EUR 490 / 990 drill hypothesis merely because a competing platform exists. Competition validates budget/category. The surface audit can be free/diagnostic during validation; the paid unit remains executed verification.

## Strongest falsifier
If 10 qualified EU SaaS/cloud providers sampled from public evidence already expose coherent switching terms, export/API documentation, formats, charges and deletion guidance, and 3 buyer conversations confirm they routinely test the workflow end-to-end, narrow or kill this wedge.

## Next autonomous action
Build a 10-provider public-surface evidence set across different SaaS categories. Score only observable evidence. Do not contact anyone, create accounts, deploy a site, or touch DNS/billing without explicit approval.
