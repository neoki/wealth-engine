## Observation

The deliverable is a project document covering current mail service, mailboxes that can migrate as-is vs require intervention, licensing, workarounds, shared mailboxes, distribution lists, **public folders**, calendars/contacts, clients, DNS, third-party tools, timing and operational roles.

## Why this matters

This is stronger than another generic competitor signal. It demonstrates that a fixed-scope **assessment itself is a sellable Microsoft-ecosystem consulting SKU**, including a written migration plan and Public Folder strategy. The market does not require every assessment to be bundled invisibly into implementation.

It also suggests two possible channels if buyer validation succeeds:

1. **MSP internal tool / white-label workflow** — reduce senior-engineer presales effort and improve fixed-fee scoping.
2. **Productized assessment offer** — partner or operator sells a fixed-scope assessment directly, with PF Planner generating the evidence-backed first draft for expert review.

Microsoft Marketplace is therefore a possible later distribution surface, not an action to take now. Listing eligibility, partner requirements, economics and operational burden must be checked only after willingness-to-pay evidence exists.

## Updated commercial hypothesis

Do not anchor PF Planner at EUR 99 merely because it is software-assisted. Published human-delivered assessments cluster materially higher in this evidence set. Automation can create margin rather than force commodity pricing.

A better pricing experiment after a real pilot exists is to compare:
- per-assessment partner tooling (low hundreds), versus
- a white-label / assisted assessment whose customer-facing value can sit in the low thousands.

The MSP must retain enough value and implementation upside for the channel to be attractive.

## New interview question

Add to buyer validation:

> If this produced a review-ready migration/scoping document from your exports, would you use it only internally to save presales hours, or would you sell the assessment itself as a fixed-fee customer deliverable?

This separates **tool willingness-to-pay** from **assessment-product willingness-to-pay** and tells us which business model has the larger capture opportunity.

## Gate

No Marketplace listing, public PF Planner site, paid acquisition, billing integration or new infrastructure until the existing buyer-validation gate is crossed: at least 3 independent confirmations of meaningful expert-time savings and at least 1 real pilot / paid-intent / live-opportunity signal.
