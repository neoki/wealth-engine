# Public Folder Modernization Pre-flight — MSP sample

## The job
Turn an existing Public Folder inventory export into a guarded modernization/scoping brief before engineers commit to a migration route or quote.

PF Planner does **not** replace your scanner, migration tooling or senior engineer. It sits between discovery and execution:

`existing inventory export → normalization → evidence gaps → modernization decisions → migration risks → execution-route comparison → scoped assessment`

## Why an MSP might care
Public Folder work mixes discovery, destination decisions, permissions/mail-enablement, application dependencies, retention/compliance and tool-specific migration constraints. Much of that analysis is repeated during pre-sales and scoping.

The product hypothesis is simple: automate the repeatable first-pass reasoning so an engineer reviews exceptions and decisions rather than rebuilding the assessment from scratch.

## What you provide
A CSV/TSV export from your existing discovery process. No tenant credentials are required for this sample workflow. The adapter is vendor-neutral and preserves missing evidence as warnings rather than inventing values.

## What you get
- executive decision brief and evidence-readiness signal;
- folder-level modernization candidates and specialist-review items;
- blockers and missing-evidence list;
- migration waves;
- Microsoft-native vs MigrationWiz technical-risk comparison based only on encoded rules;
- next actions that move the engagement toward validated scope, effort, dates and quote.

## Guardrails
This is planning output, not autonomous migration authorization. A lower route-risk score is not a purchasing or execution decision. Licensing, commercial terms, throughput, undocumented edge cases and engineering judgement remain outside the current model. Compliance holds, application dependencies and weak evidence stay visible for specialist review.

## Validation question
Would this artifact materially reduce the engineering/pre-sales time you spend turning an inventory into a migration scope? If yes, the useful pilot is one anonymized real inventory and a comparison of **time spent + issues caught + corrections required** against your normal workflow.

## Pilot success criterion
Proceed only if independent MSPs confirm meaningful scoping/pre-sales savings and at least one is willing to use the workflow on a real opportunity, pilot it, or pay for it. Until then, do not invest in a public product site.
