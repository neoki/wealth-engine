# Sweep 10 — solar maturation + fresh WORLD — 2026-09-19

## Solar-maintenance-admin-channel-operator
Classification: POSSIBLE-ROTATED.

Evidence rounds completed:
1. Spain price/demand: residential maintenance is already sold nationally at €15.90/month or €152.90/year including an annual visit, inspection and cleaning.
2. Administrator-channel saturation: specialist providers already coordinate directly with property administrators, issue community invoices and photo/technical reports. The proposed orchestration/reporting wedge is therefore already part of the service itself.
3. Margin/renewal geometry: at ~€153/year residential pricing, inserting another orchestration layer between buyer and qualified technician leaves little obvious gross-margin room unless Rockrai owns privileged distribution or bundles multiple maintenance categories. No such privileged channel is currently evidenced.

Decision: rotate, do not build. Revisit only if a privileged administrator portfolio/distribution relationship appears or if a multi-service bundle materially changes economics.

## Fresh WORLD observations

### 1. Weekend/festive bin handling for apartment communities
A Madrid operator sells a strikingly narrow recurring service: take communal bins out and return them on weekends/holidays, from €50/community/month. Other operators confirm the category and adjacent upsells (door-to-door bag collection, bin-room cleaning, large-community container movement). This is an excellent example of synchronization-anchor route density: every customer needs the task at predictable municipal collection times.

Status: OBSERVATION → POSSIBLE for geographic-copy search, not yet a Galicia candidate. Need municipality-specific collection rules, existing caretaker/cleaning substitution, achievable portals/hour and administrator distribution.

### 2. Certified confidential-document destruction
Current Spanish offers show €55–95 for up to ~1m3, and periodic service as low as €13.50–21/month depending on geography/frequency. A Coruña already has a specialist with Galicia-wide coverage. Demand and compliance are real, but local entry is occupied and ticket is low. More interesting mutation: expiry-triggered archival destruction for asesorías/legal/medical businesses, where software identifies retention expiry and batches certified collection. Keep as WORLD primitive, not candidate yet.

### 3. Office plant renting
Spain has an established plant-rental model including installation, maintenance and replacement; franchise evidence describes weekly maintenance and a €25k franchise investment. Proven recurring B2B service but inventory/care/route intensity is high. No elevation.

### 4. Pickup → specialist transformation → return
The Alfombrator primitive generalizes: sneaker/shoe cleaning, electronics repair and laundry already use pickup/return. Current sneaker cleaning ranges from ~€15.50 to €55/pair, with free pickup often requiring basket thresholds. This reinforces the engine rule: do not copy the wrapper alone; search for high-ticket categories where specialist supply is fragmented and logistics are cheap relative to transformation value.

## Engine learning
A narrow service can be attractive when an external schedule synchronizes demand. Municipal collection calendars, building routines, annual compliance dates and other fixed events can manufacture route density. Search for `tiny mandatory/repetitive task + synchronized schedule + fragmented responsibility + B2B2C aggregator` rather than generic route services.

## Next
Fresh WORLD should deliberately search synchronized micro-services outside solar/cleaning: fixed inspection dates, handoff windows, replenishment cycles, compliance expiries, event teardown/setup, recurring physical verification. Mature the bin-handling copy only if a target geography shows the same operational need and enough portal density.
