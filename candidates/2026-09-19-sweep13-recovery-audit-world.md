# Sweep 13 — Recovery-value WORLD perturbation

Date: 2026-09-19

## Directive
Avoid conventional waste/routes. Search unrelated domains for stranded value plus an annoying/mandatory recovery task, while forcing unrelated WORLD observations.

## Signals sampled

### 1. Corporate guarantees / deposits recovery — proven, occupied
Spanish specialist SOTAM already offers recovery/cancellation of expired guarantees, cash deposits and surety instruments, with software, operational handling and pricing combining fixed management fees plus a variable recovery-linked fee. Banco de España guidance confirms that guarantee cancellation can stop risk commissions when extinction of risk is properly evidenced. This validates the stranded-value + bureaucratic-release pattern but gives no obvious entry wedge.

Classification: OBSERVED-OCCUPIED.

### 2. Accounts-payable recovery audit — proven; SME/channel mutation worth maturation
Spanish providers already audit historical AP/procurement data to find duplicate payments, overpayments, missed credits and contract discrepancies, often on success-fee economics and across several prior years. Spanish SME-oriented material also describes duplicate payments and overpayments as real AP failure modes.

Mutation: **Recovery Audit Lite for Spanish SMEs via accounting/advisory firms**.

Instead of selling enterprise consulting, the advisor/accounting firm already holding the ledger exports a bounded historical dataset (initially supplier ledger + invoice/payment export). Software flags high-confidence recoverable anomalies; human/advisor validates; recovery can be offered success-fee or shared-savings. Distribution is B2B2B through advisors rather than direct CAC. The asset is money already belonging economically to the client; the annoying lifecycle task is proving and reclaiming it.

Why it survives one round:
- demand mechanism is proven by established recovery-audit firms;
- success-fee model reduces buyer risk;
- SMEs have the same error classes but enterprise recovery-audit delivery appears relatively consultancy-heavy;
- accounting/advisory firms can provide a privileged distribution/data channel and share upside;
- an initial falsification artifact can be offline/read-only: no banking access, no autonomous payment action.

Risks / unknowns:
- recoverable euros per SME may be too small to support human recovery work;
- modern ERPs may catch enough duplicates prospectively that historical recovery is sparse;
- data-processing/confidentiality and advisor professional obligations need careful design;
- incumbent audit/recovery firms may already serve this segment through partners even if web evidence is weak;
- success-fee collection and supplier-contact workflow may create operational friction.

Next gates: quantify recoverable € distribution by SME invoice volume; determine minimum viable client size; map Spanish advisor/accounting-firm channel competition; test whether Sage/A3/other common exports contain enough data for a read-only scan; clarify legal/professional framing before any client-facing test.

Classification: POSSIBLE+ after round 1.

### 3. SaaS/license spend recovery — occupied
Spain/EU providers already discover unused seats, renewal leakage and redundant SaaS, with reported savings claims and consolidated procurement. No entry wedge identified.

Classification: OBSERVED-OCCUPIED.

### 4. Telecom cost audit — mature/occupied
Spanish consultancies offer free telecom audits, negotiation and ongoing invoice control to SMEs, some reporting thousands of clients and ~30% savings claims. Strong proof of savings-funded distribution, weak room for generic entry.

Classification: OBSERVED-OCCUPIED.

### 5. Surplus construction materials — increasingly occupied
Multiple marketplaces already monetize leftover construction stock and secondary materials, including Spain-focused entrants. The latent asset is obvious, but marketplace liquidity is the hard part and no privileged channel emerged.

Classification: OBSERVED-OCCUPIED.

### 6. Ticket/voucher resale — occupied
Multiple EU/Spain-facing resale systems already handle unwanted tickets with verification/escrow/face-value mechanisms. No reason to enter generically.

Classification: OBSERVED-OCCUPIED.

### 7. Freight invoice recovery — strong foreign proof, retain as primitive
AI-first freight-audit companies now audit every invoice against contracts, generate disputes and charge on recovered value. This is a clean example of `structured spend + contractual truth + detectable leakage + automated claim packet + success fee`. Spain-specific room was not established in this pass.

Classification: WORLD-PRIMITIVE; do not promote yet.

### 8. Supplier-credit / rebate recovery — strong adjacent primitive
Modern services join PO, invoice, goods-receipt and warehouse evidence, keep supplier claims open until credit is actually reconciled, and automate rebate/refund discovery. This suggests recovery audit can extend beyond duplicate invoices into `contract-to-cash-back` loops, but broad scope would be premature.

Classification: WORLD-PRIMITIVE.

## Engine learning candidate
A more general opportunity primitive emerged:

`money/value already economically owed to buyer + fragmented evidence + tedious claim/cancellation process + machine-detectable discrepancy + performance pricing + privileged data-holder channel`.

This is stronger than generic 'latent asset' because it does not require resale liquidity. Search future WORLD sweeps for narrow vertical instances where recovery value is large relative to verification/claim cost and incumbents do not already own the channel.

## Decision
Promote only `sme-ap-recovery-audit-via-advisors` into the active queue as POSSIBLE+ for independent maturation. Do not build yet. Rotate away from recovery-audit variants in the next WORLD sweep to prevent a new attractor.
