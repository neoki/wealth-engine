# Green-Claim Evidence Reconciler

Status: **REJECTED AS GENERIC PRODUCT — 2026-09-18**

## Signal validation
Directive (EU) 2024/825 (Empowering Consumers for the Green Transition) is real and applies from 27 September 2026. The Commission states Member States had to transpose by 27 March 2026 and the rules apply from 27 September 2026. The June 2026 CPC common understanding also addresses old-stock enforcement/transitional realities.

This corrects the earlier loose framing: this is the Empowering Consumers Directive / EmpCo regime; it should not be conflated with the separately debated Green Claims Directive.

## Proposed job
`published/draft claim -> detect -> evidence -> assess -> approve -> audit trail -> reusable substantiation`

## Competition test
The supposed workflow gap is already directly occupied:
- ClaimBasis: ingest product content, detect risky environmental claims, link evidence, selectively publish substantiation.
- Verdanox: claims register, evidence management, multi-role approvals, version history, audit trail and verifier-ready export.
- Mossy: find risky claims, connect evidence, remediate and retain an audit trail.
- Maska: analyzes claims, links dated/traceable evidence and supports compliant communication.
- PocketSeed: living claim/evidence source of truth and consumer-facing credentials.
- EcoAppraise/Gilbert EmpCo: automated green-claim regulatory screening and evidence-gap analysis.
- Qiro: open-source claim review gate with source-linked evidence status.

Competition strongly validates the pain and impending deadline. Rejection is NOT because competition exists; it is because our generic thesis does not currently offer a distinct data asset, workflow, distribution advantage or underserved buyer that justifies another horizontal product.

## Lateral connections retained
1. **Old-stock remediation economics**: prioritize which existing SKUs/packaging/assets to relabel, sell through, withdraw or remediate under transitional enforcement. This is an operations/inventory decision rather than another claim scanner.
2. **Supplier-evidence expiry / propagation**: one supplier certificate or LCA can underpin claims across many SKUs/channels; changes/expiry could create a graph of downstream claims requiring review.
3. **Agency portfolio layer**: agencies managing many brands may need cross-client remediation queues and proof of completion, but existing governance platforms may already extend here; buyer evidence required before promotion.
4. **Greenhushing opportunity**: identify defensible claims a company is failing to communicate. UnitMode explicitly markets this, so it is validated but not whitespace by itself.

## Decision
Do not build or deploy. Rotate to the next candidate. Revisit only if external evidence reveals a narrower operational wedge with superior distribution or economics.
