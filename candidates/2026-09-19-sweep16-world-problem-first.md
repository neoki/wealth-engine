# Sweep 16 — problem-first WORLD breadth

Date: 2026-09-19

## Method
Fresh problem-first sampling across hospitality, education/family administration, HR/SME operations, automotive services, product compliance and niche commerce. Recovery/refund/waste/routes/bereavement were not used as seeds.

## Findings

### Hotel lost-property return — occupied
The job is real: front desks receive calls/emails, must register found items, verify ownership and arrange shipping. But several current products already package intake, matching, guest communication, chain-of-custody and courier return for hotels/venues. No clear wedge found.
Classification: `observed-occupied`.

### School/AMPA trip/activity money and permissions — occupied
The pain is real and institutionally dense, but current association/school products already combine family records, communications, activities, payments and reminders. No reason to build a generic entrant.
Classification: `observed-occupied`.

### Wedding/event QR photo collection — saturated commodity
Numerous products offer no-app QR upload, private galleries, downloads and one-off pricing/free tiers. Do not enter generically.
Classification: `observed-crowded`.

### SME HR/onboarding — occupied suite market
Current SME HR products already bundle onboarding/time/employee management. Generic onboarding is not a candidate.
Classification: `observed-occupied`.

### Mobile detailing subscription — maturation round
Spain now shows explicit mobile detailing operators plus monthly/weekly maintenance clubs; Galicia has a mobile operator covering the region. The model is therefore proven, not hypothetical. However the differentiator is operational density and service quality, not software, and low-price tunnel subscriptions create a cheap substitute for routine exterior cleaning. No privileged acquisition node or dense fleet/office/residential channel has been demonstrated for us.
Decision: rotate rather than build.
Classification: `possible-rotated`.
Next gate: privileged dense node (fleet, premium residential parking, office parking, dealership) with pre-aggregated vehicles and credible operator economics.

### Digital Product Passport — strong exogenous wave, generic tooling crowded
The EU DPP Registry and test environment went live in July 2026. The Commission identifies product-category rollout including batteries first and priority work for textiles, tyres, aluminium, furniture, ICT and energy-related products. This is a genuine external structural wave. But the generic DPP SaaS layer is already crowded, including SME-focused textile offerings, Spanish providers, supplier portals, AI document extraction and GS1/QR tooling.

The potentially interesting unsolved layer is not another passport generator. The recurring operational burden appears upstream: manufacturers/brands need to collect structured evidence from many suppliers, map heterogeneous PDFs/Excel/certificates into product-level data, chase missing fields and preserve provenance as requirements evolve. Existing DPP vendors themselves advertise supplier portals and document extraction, which validates the job but also means the wedge is not yet proven.

Candidate: `dpp-supplier-evidence-operations-channel`.
Classification: `possible`.
Hypothesis: sell an evidence-collection/normalization workflow through compliance consultancies, industry associations, ERP/accounting/quality partners or as a service layer feeding whichever DPP platform the manufacturer chooses; avoid competing as the passport registry/system of record.
Next gate: prove that SME manufacturers have a repeated supplier-evidence collection/chasing burden that remains poorly served by current DPP platforms, identify one narrow product category with imminent enough obligation, and establish willingness to pay/channel economics. Do not build yet.

## Durable observation
A regulation/platform wave can be attractive while its obvious software artifact is already commoditizing. Search one layer upstream for the recurring data/evidence acquisition work required to feed every competing compliance platform. This is only a hypothesis until repeated in other domains.

## Next
Sweep 17 should keep unrelated WORLD breadth and give `dpp-supplier-evidence-operations-channel` one materially different maturation round focused on buyer workflow, incumbent supplier-portal coverage, deadline timing and channel economics. Do not turn DPP into a new regulatory attractor.
