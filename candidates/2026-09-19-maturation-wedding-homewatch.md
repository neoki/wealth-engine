# Maturation — Wedding Content Network vs Home Watch — 2026-09-19

## Wedding Content Creator network / Galicia

### Round A — demand and price
- Galicia recorded 9,348 marriages in 2024; Pontevedra province alone recorded 3,382. 2025 Q3 had 3,543 marriages in Galicia, +1.72% YoY.
- Current Spanish WCC offers span a very wide price band: P2P €250–320; Team Bride/Lala from €490; Say I Do Social €500/€900; Ventura €1,200 + VAT; Ram a l'aire €1,028.50–1,573. This validates real willingness to pay but also indicates an immature category without settled pricing.
- Traditional Galician wedding video starts around €1,250 at Visualtec and delivery is typically 3–6 months, strengthening WCC's immediate-delivery wedge rather than requiring it to replace professional video.

### Round B — distribution
- Spain already has wedding supplier directories/aggregators (Bodas.net, Team Bride) and planners/venues that can act as channels. Team Bride has a national WCC category; Bodas.net has individual WCC listings. This lowers category-education cost but also means distribution is not empty.
- A network has a real structural advantage over solo creators: geographic coverage, backup creator when someone is ill, standardized deliverables, availability across simultaneous dates, and channel partnerships. This is the candidate wedge—not inventing WCC.

### Round C — economics / scalability
- The market price band permits a hypothetical agency structure, but creator payout/CAC are not publicly established enough to claim attractive margins yet.
- Seasonality and Saturday concentration create both a constraint and a network advantage: a solo creator caps out at one wedding/date; a network can parallelize.
- Key unknown: whether trained creator supply can be recruited at a payout that leaves >=30–40% gross contribution after editing/QA/acquisition. Do not call BRILLIANT until this is evidenced.

### Classification
PROMISING, strengthened. Not BRILLIANT yet.
Reason: paid category + low capex + visible local market + network scalability, but margin and acquisition are still assumptions.

## Second-home Home Watch / Spain

### Round A — demand and geography
- Alicante is a much stronger launch geometry than Galicia. Current evidence puts foreign buyers at ~46.4% of Alicante transactions in Q2 2026 and non-resident share around one third; Costa Blanca therefore has dense absent-owner demand.
- Galicia foreign purchases are growing (1,109 in H2 2025, +6.6% YoY), but national non-resident foreign purchases are overwhelmingly concentrated in Comunidad Valenciana and Andalucía. Galicia is not the obvious first market.

### Round B — price and competition
- Current Spanish operators prove recurring spend but also show strong price competition: Torrevieja €39/89/229 per month; KwiCo €30/55; Home Care Touch €99/169/299; Axarquía €100/200/400; Home Watch Costa from €59/month plus keyholding; Second House Solutions €65/month.
- Competition is positive demand evidence. However, the low end is already cheap enough that a pure 'monthly inspection + photos + keys' clone has weak margin unless route density and upsells are excellent.

### Round C — economics / moat
- Route density is decisive. At €55/month, one monthly visit with travel/reporting can become labor-heavy quickly; economics improve through dense neighborhoods, 2+ visits routed together, keyholding, technician access, repairs/maintenance coordination and higher-value memberships.
- Trust, local presence, multilingual service and estate-agent/developer/referral channels matter more than software. Liability can be bounded contractually to visual inspections, as existing operators do, but insurance/legal setup still needs checking before production.
- The best version may be B2B2C: partner with agencies/developers selling to foreign buyers and become the post-sale property-care layer, rather than acquire homeowners one by one with ads.

### Classification
PROMISING, but narrowed to **B2B2C post-sale property care for foreign/non-resident owners in Costa Blanca-type markets**. Not BRILLIANT yet.
Reason: recurring demand and dense target market are proven; commoditized inspection pricing and local operational burden make acquisition channel/unit economics decisive.

## Portfolio decision
Neither candidate reaches BRILLIANT on desk evidence alone. Do not deploy a public experiment yet. Wedding Content currently has the better capital-light test geometry; Home Watch has better recurrence but substantially heavier local operations.
