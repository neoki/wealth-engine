# Home Organization maturation + WORLD sweep 07 — 2026-09-19

## Home organization: final desk round
Event-driven professional organization is real, but the Spanish entry geometry is weaker than the initial Mallorca luxury signal suggested.

Evidence:
- Kdó (Madrid) publicly prices move organization from €600 standard and €1,000 premium.
- Calma Organisers prices relocation/moving from €540.
- Ordinary moving companies already bundle packing/unpacking/logistics and compete at roughly €299–€1,500 for complete moves, compressing the organizer's standalone value.
- Mallorca luxury remains exceptional: Home Atelier publishes €1,100–€3,000 arrival reset and €1,500–€4,000+ projects.

Classification: POSSIBLE-ROTATED. The attractive version is luxury/expat/second-home arrival reset or complex downsizing, not generic household organization. No more desk research unless a low-CAC referral channel or high-ticket geography appears.

## Fresh WORLD sampling
This pass deliberately sampled unrelated physical/local businesses rather than extending weddings/home organization.

### 1. Grave-care subscription / cemetery concierge
Observed model:
- Campa.care publicly sells recurring grave care at €35/€70/€125 per month with cleaning, flowers, photo proof and app tracking.
- Jardín del Recuerdo sells a €38/month monthly plan with cleaning, fresh flowers and photo documentation.
- Mármoles Artesanos offers monthly/quarterly/semiannual/annual maintenance for families unable to travel.
- TeleROSA sells cemetery floral arrangements nationally from roughly €30 to €190 depending on format.

Interpretation: proven recurring willingness to pay, emotionally important job, naturally remote customer, simple proof-of-work, and route-density economics. Spain already has supply, so the opportunity is not invention. Geographic/segment copying may still work. Galicia is an especially interesting hypothesis because diaspora/remote-family behavior could fit the job, but this needs actual local supply/demand and route economics before promotion.

Classification: POSSIBLE+; next rounds = Galicia/local supply gap, cemetery route density, flower/material gross margin, acquisition channel (funeral homes/marble shops/florists/diaspora search).

### 2. Mobile premium car detailing subscription
Observed Spanish market:
- LuxWash Costa del Sol: €150 full detail, €250 deep interior, €600/month VIP for four premium washes.
- Super Clean Mallorca: maintenance from €120/month and signature recurring care from €400/month.
- Easy Deal: mobile full detailing from €150.
- Multiple competitors serve Costa del Sol/Mallorca.

Interpretation: subscription and high-ticket demand are validated, but obvious luxury geographies are already crowded and execution requires equipment/technicians. Retain only as copy candidate for underserved affluent micro-geographies or fleet/B2B channel.

Classification: POSSIBLE, not priority.

### 3. Airbnb linen/laundry route
WashMe Costa del Sol publishes €6.49–€6.99/kg residential, €3.75–€5.75/kg wholesale, recurring pickup routes and linen rental; turnover pricing ranges about €28–€85 by property size. Guest-Home separately prices cleaning/linen management by departure.

Interpretation: recurring B2B route economics and obvious pain, but capital/operations/laundry infrastructure are heavier than desired. Interesting primitive is linen-as-a-service to small property managers, not owning a laundromat.

Classification: POSSIBLE-low-capital only if fulfillment can be subcontracted; otherwise rotate.

### 4. Soft-play/event equipment rental
Madrid Soft Play sells event packages from €250/€320; other Spanish inflatable rentals start much lower. Galicia has established fixed party venues, but this pass did not establish a clear mobile soft-play supply gap.

Interpretation: proven rental economics and reusable inventory, but requires capex/storage/transport and has local competition. Keep as raw observation, not active candidate.

## Engine learning
The strongest new primitive is not software: recurring remote-care + physical proof + route density. Grave care has the same attractive economic structure as Home Watch but potentially higher emotional willingness-to-pay and much shorter service stops. It deserves maturation, not production yet.
