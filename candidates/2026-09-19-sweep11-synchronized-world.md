# Sweep 11 — synchronized micro-services + fresh WORLD — 2026-09-19

## Synchronized-community-bin-handling-copy
Classification: POSSIBLE-ROTATED.

Second pass tested the exact gates set by sweep 10: target-geography need, route economics, administrator distribution and incumbent substitution.

Evidence:
- The model is clearly real in Madrid: Cubos Express advertises weekend/holiday take-out-and-return from €50/community/month, while Don Cubo offers daily, weekend, bag-down, bin-cleaning and community-cleaning variants.
- The search did not produce equivalent evidence of a Galicia-specific standalone bin-handling category. That is not proof of absence, but it means the geographic-copy thesis lacks target-market validation.
- Galicia already has integrated community-management/cleaning operators such as Serga. Generic community-cleaning offers also bundle rubbish removal/bin cleaning at low ticket. The likely substitute is therefore an existing cleaner/caretaker rather than 'doing nothing'.
- At €50/portal/month, attractive economics require unusually dense routing and many portals per administrator relationship. We have no evidence of either in the target geography.

Decision: rotate. Do not build. Revisit only if an administrator portfolio exposes an explicit weekend/holiday service gap or a dense municipality produces evidence of many uncovered portals.

## Queue probes

### Pet-waste-removal subscription
Real recurring model confirmed nearby in Portugal: Dog Yard Patrol sells weekly garden cleanup from €60/month for one dog and €80/month for two. International operators also sell apartment/HOA/dog-park contracts. This validates the primitive, but not Spain/Galicia distribution. DTC detached-home routing is likely sparse; the potentially better mutation is B2B concentrated dog areas (residential complexes, dog daycare, parks), where one stop aggregates demand.
Status: POSSIBLE, retain for one concentration/channel pass rather than DTC build.

### Senior-family-tech-concierge
Spanish supply is already strong and cheap: Blue Services advertises senior-focused 24/7 tech support with unlimited in-person visits at €39.99/year promotional annual pricing; CaixaBank and specialist providers bundle broader senior protection/technology. A generic 'help parents with technology' subscription therefore has weak pricing power and incumbent distribution disadvantages.
Status: POSSIBLE-ROTATED. Revisit only with a sharply different high-value job/channel.

### Sell-it-for-me
Revalia already automates multi-marketplace publication for second-hand shops. This does not kill a full-service consignment model, but software-only cross-posting is occupied. The interesting question is whether a vertical can justify physical intake, pricing, photography, listing, negotiation, shipping and commission. No elevation this sweep.

## Fresh WORLD observations

### 1. Waste value can subsidize an otherwise paid service
Used cooking oil is not merely a disposal task. FuelNature advertises used-oil collection plus free hood cleaning; Galicia's SB Galicia explicitly pays for oil or exchanges its value for cleaning products, pest control, grease-separator maintenance, and filter/hood cleaning. Recycas and other Galician operators already combine oil collection with kitchen maintenance.

This is a stronger reusable primitive than 'recurring route': `valuable by-product/residual stream -> recurring collection relationship -> use recovered value to subsidize an adjacent painful service -> bundle creates retention and route density`.

The oil/hood instance itself is OCCUPIED in Galicia and should not be copied blindly. Search other residual streams where the generator currently pays for an adjacent service and the residual has recoverable value.

### 2. Compliance-maintenance subscriptions are real but often specialist-locked
Fire-protection maintenance is sold around €200–900/year with periodic certified checks; DEA maintenance around €249/year; both require qualified/specialist execution. These are demand anchors, but orchestration alone is weak when the specialist already owns scheduling, certification and renewal.
Learning: external compliance calendars manufacture recurrence, but not necessarily an intermediary margin. Look for fragmented compliance bundles sharing the same buyer/site, not one more layer on a single specialist service.

### 3. Linen-as-infrastructure for property managers
Current Spanish operators rent linen, collect dirty stock, wash, track and redeliver synchronized to turnover calendars. One Costa del Sol operator claims 200+ properties at launch and RFID/instant-swap positioning; Madrid offers per-stay linen rental around €30 for a two-bed example. This validates the 'synchronized inventory loop' primitive but the category is already emerging. Keep as pattern, not candidate.

## Engine learning
Synchronization alone is insufficient. The strongest physical-service geometries can stack multiple economic engines: synchronized demand + node density + recoverable residual value + adjacent service substitution. Conversely, compliance recurrence without ownership of distribution or execution often just strengthens the incumbent specialist.
