# Construction Variation-to-Cash Gap Auditor

Status: rejected as a horizontal product
Date: 2026-09-18

## Hypothesis
Reconstruct extra work that actually happened but never entered the commercial workflow by scanning site diaries/daily logs, RFIs, email, meeting minutes, drawings, photos and timesheets; map the event to contractual entitlement; draft/quantify the missing potential change order or notice; then carry it toward certification/invoicing. The wedge was retrospective or continuous recovery of stranded revenue rather than ordinary change-order administration.

## Falsification result
Exact-match products already exist.

- DOBS AI Construction states that an AI agent continuously scans daily logs, emails and RFI responses for signals of extra work and drafts defensible PCOs with narrative and supporting photos/logs/emails. It explicitly positions this as change-order capture and margin protection.
- Magra Radar continuously monitors email, daily logs, RFIs, meeting minutes and schedule updates, identifies potential change orders/claims/backcharges/delay events, checks entitlement against the contract, drafts notices/CORs, prices impact, tracks response and carries the event through resolution/payment. This closes almost the entire hypothesized loop.
- DailyLogsPro, Konstryx, BizFlowAI and WarTable provide additional market validation around converting field evidence/undocumented extras into change orders and billing.

This is stronger than adjacent competition: Magra and DOBS implement the core detection-from-unstructured-project-records mechanism that was supposed to differentiate the candidate. Therefore competition validates both pain and willingness to pay, but there is no sufficiently distinct horizontal wedge to justify building another product now.

## Decision
REJECT horizontal product. Do not build or deploy.

## Lateral learning
The higher-level `revenue-capture-before-optimization` thesis survives and is strengthened: construction vendors explicitly sell recovery/protection of margin from already-performed or entitlement-bearing work. But `unrecorded work -> evidence -> entitlement -> recoverable cash` is already productized in construction, so Wealth Engine should seek the same economic geometry in domains where operational evidence and commercial entitlement are still disconnected.

A useful discovery lens is `money that exists before the software`: revenue, reimbursement, credit, rebate, entitlement or production capacity already earned/available but stranded because an operational event never crossed into the financial workflow.

Next candidate: operational-credential-revenue-blocker.
