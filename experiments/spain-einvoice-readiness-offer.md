# Spain VeriFactu Readiness — productized diagnostic

For Spanish advisory/accounting firms and SMEs that need to know **whether each invoicing workflow will actually survive the 2027 VeriFactu deadlines** without blindly replacing the ERP.

## Why this wedge now
- VeriFactu is the near-term forcing function: 1 Jan 2027 for Corporate Income Tax taxpayers and 1 Jul 2027 for the remaining affected taxpayers (RDL 15/2025).
- Spain B2B e-invoicing is a separate regime. RD 238/2026 is approved, but effective deadlines depend on a still-pending ministerial technical order. Do not market both as one deadline.
- This distinction itself is a buyer pain: firms must classify clients/workflows correctly before recommending software changes.

## The decision we resolve
For one company workflow or advisory-firm client archetype:
1. Is the workflow in scope for VeriFactu, B2B e-invoicing, both eventually, or neither?
2. Which system actually *issues* the invoice versus merely accounts for it?
3. Is the issuing SIF ready for the applicable VeriFactu operating model and deadline?
4. What evidence is missing from the vendor / current stack?
5. What must change now, what should be tested, and what can safely wait for the B2B technical order?

## Deliverable
- workflow/system inventory
- applicability and deadline map
- vendor-evidence checklist (version, declaration/responsibility evidence where applicable, QR/record-flow capabilities, integration dependencies)
- red/amber/green gap map
- prioritized remediation plan
- one-page management decision summary

## Initial buyer
**Advisory/accounting firms with many heterogeneous SME client stacks.** One diagnostic template can be reused across dozens or hundreds of clients, giving distribution and repeated learning that a single-SME sale does not.

## Commercial experiment
Do not lead with a €79 consumer-like audit. Validate two value units:
- per-client diagnostic: tentative €149–€299
- advisor portfolio triage / workshop: tentative €750–€1,500 depending on client archetypes

These are hypotheses, not published prices. No paid acquisition or public checkout before buyer evidence.

## Validation protocol
A useful buyer signal is not "interesting". Require an advisor to provide an anonymized current invoicing workflow/client archetype and judge whether the diagnostic:
- prevents a wrong software recommendation,
- surfaces missing vendor evidence,
- reduces advisor review time,
- can be reused across multiple clients.

### Gate
PASS to productization only after >=3 independent advisor validations and >=1 real anonymized client workflow/pilot or explicit willingness to pay. Until then, fulfil manually from supplied evidence and do not build SaaS.

## Expansion path if validated
VeriFactu readiness -> advisor portfolio readiness -> combined Spanish invoicing transition workspace when the B2B technical order makes that second deadline operational.
