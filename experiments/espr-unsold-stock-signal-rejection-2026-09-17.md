# ESPR unsold-stock / destruction evidence — signal rejection

Date: 2026-09-17
Status: REJECT NEW EXPERIMENT — strong problem, insufficient wedge

## Signal

EU ESPR rules are operational now for large apparel/footwear companies: the destruction ban applies from 19 July 2026. Companies relying on derogations need supporting evidence, records must be retained for five years, and annual public disclosure is required. A standardised disclosure format applies from February 2027.

Primary sources:
- European Commission, 17 Jul 2026: https://environment.ec.europa.eu/news/ban-destruction-unsold-clothes-and-shoes-enters-application-2026-07-17_en
- European Commission, 9 Feb 2026: https://environment.ec.europa.eu/news/new-eu-rules-stop-destruction-unsold-clothes-and-shoes-2026-02-09_en

This initially suggested a product such as `ESPR Unsold Stock Evidence Preflight`: ingest ERP/WMS/disposal exports, identify unsupported destruction routes, reconcile CN codes/quantities/reasons/evidence and produce the standardised disclosure dataset.

## Competition check changed the decision

The market signal is real, but an exact wedge already exists. FINDS publicly positions itself around the claim that the destruction ban is a *defensible routing decision*, not merely a logging problem; it sits above ERP/WMS and captures routing logic, evidence and auditability. That overlaps the most attractive differentiation we could otherwise claim.

Other adjacent offerings include returns/circular-routing providers such as ReBound, generic ESPR compliance services, disclosure trackers and DPP platforms.

Sources:
- FINDS: https://finds.solutions/espr/
- ReBound: https://www.reboundreturns.com/sustainability/espr/

Competition is positive evidence of spend and urgency; it is **not** itself a kill criterion. The rejection is narrower: after competitor discovery, the proposed product does not yet have a materially different buyer, workflow, proprietary input, distribution advantage, or economic model.

## Decision

Do **not** create a web experiment, landing page or another generic ESPR scanner/preflight now.

Do **not** discard the market. Keep it as a monitored signal. Re-open only if evidence reveals one of these wedges:

1. a data source competitors do not ingest (e.g. disposal contractor evidence reconciliation at portfolio scale),
2. a channel with repeated portfolios (auditor, waste operator, returns platform, fashion ERP/WMS integrator),
3. a narrow cross-system failure that existing routing products demonstrably miss,
4. a substantially cheaper automated verification layer that can be sold to existing ESPR vendors rather than against them.

## Wealth Engine learning

A regulatory deadline + painful workflow + visible competitors is not sufficient to spawn an experiment. Before creating a new candidate, perform an **exact-wedge collision check**:

`problem validation → incumbent/competitor discovery → exact workflow comparison → channel/data advantage check → only then experiment`

Competition should raise confidence that money exists while simultaneously forcing sharper differentiation. If the exact workflow is already productised, Wealth Engine should search for a complement, channel or verification layer rather than reflexively build a clone.

No web property, Railway service, DNS change, paid service or financial action was created for this rejected signal.
