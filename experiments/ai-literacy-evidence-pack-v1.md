# AI Literacy Evidence Pack — validation v1

Status: manual-validation

## Thesis

EU organisations increasingly use generative AI, while the practical record of staff guidance, training and AI usage is often fragmented. The current EU AI Act framework creates a potential productised job: turn actual AI usage, roles and existing literacy measures into a small, dated evidence pack that management can maintain.

This is not an official certification and must not promise legal compliance.

## Buyer hypothesis

Primary targets:
- Microsoft / AI consultancies deploying Copilot and related tools for clients.
- Professional-services firms already using generative AI.

The higher-leverage channel may be partner enablement: one consultancy could reuse the workflow across many customers.

## Manual deliverable

Input:
1. AI systems actually used.
2. Roles using them.
3. Use contexts.
4. Existing policies and training.
5. Existing attendance / acknowledgement evidence.

Output:
- AI-use inventory snapshot.
- Role x AI-use matrix.
- Literacy-measures gap map: PRESENT / PARTIAL / UNKNOWN.
- Proportionate literacy plan by role/context.
- Attendance register template.
- Evidence index with owner, date and review date.
- One-page management summary.
- Items requiring specialist review.

## Wedge

Do not sell "another AI course". Training is already crowded. Test whether evidence assembly, role proportionality and repeatability are the valuable missing layer.

Competition is evidence of spend, not an automatic reason to abandon the idea.

## Pricing hypotheses

Interview anchors only, not public offers:
- single-company manual pack: EUR 250–750
- partner portfolio setup / reusable workflow: EUR 1,000–3,000

Do not create checkout or paid service automatically.

## Validation

Ask 5 Microsoft/AI consultancies or professional-services firms:
1. After an AI deployment, who owns the literacy evidence?
2. What evidence do you currently leave behind?
3. Is it role/context-specific or mainly generic training evidence?
4. Would a generated pack save meaningful consulting/admin time or fill a missing deliverable?
5. Would you test it on one anonymised customer deployment?
6. Would you prefer per-customer, white-label or embedded delivery?

## Gate

PASS only with:
- at least 3 independent buyers confirming the evidence problem, and
- at least 1 anonymised real workflow, pilot or explicit willingness to pay, and
- evidence of at least 1 hour of professional work saved OR a currently omitted deliverable being filled.

If buyers say generic training records already solve the problem and evidence assembly is not painful, kill this wedge rather than building software.

## Expansion only after validation

AI Literacy Evidence Pack -> AI Usage Inventory -> lightweight AI governance evidence workspace.

Do not build the expansion before the wedge validates.

## Research notes — 2026-09-16

The Article 4 obligation remains in the amended 2026 framework, but current wording is softer and context-dependent; there is no official individual AI-literacy certificate or fixed curriculum. Customer-facing legal wording must therefore be checked against primary EU text before publication.

Market signals reviewed include Barcelona Code School AI compliance training, RegulatoryAI Article 4 materials and RegShelf's literacy evidence kit. These establish competition and buyer attention; they do not by themselves validate willingness to pay for our proposed evidence-pack wedge.
