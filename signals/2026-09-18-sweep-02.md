## Fresh candidate queue (max 5)

### 1 — CRA Incident Evidence Pack / Submission Reconciler
Buyer: EU software/device manufacturers, especially smaller vendors and security/compliance providers serving portfolios.
Wedge: incident/vulnerability evidence → CRA classification/timeline → required notification artefacts → submission-status/audit pack. Do not build another vulnerability scanner or generic CRA checklist.
Why now: reporting obligation became operational 2026-09-11 and applies to installed products.
Distribution hypothesis: security consultancies, MSP/MSSP, product-security firms can repeat across clients.
Next falsification: map official reporting workflow and competitors; determine whether existing GRC/PSIRT platforms already produce the exact regulator-ready chain.

### 2 — Green-Claim Evidence Reconciler
Buyer: ecommerce agencies, brands and legal/compliance teams with many live SKUs/pages.
Wedge: crawl existing claims → link each environmental claim/label to substantiating evidence and permitted wording → exception queue + proof pack. Not a generic ESG writer.
Why now: EU rules apply 2026-09-27 to claims already in market.
Distribution hypothesis: agencies can run portfolio-wide reviews repeatedly across clients.
Next falsification: inspect enforcement requirements and current green-claims scanners; test whether evidence reconciliation is underserved.

### 3 — Asesoría E-Invoice Client Portfolio Migration Map
Buyer: Spanish asesorías/accounting firms with tens/hundreds of client companies.
Wedge: client × invoicing software × readiness × migration route × blocker × deadline → portfolio operating queue. Not another invoicing app and not a generic readiness checklist.
Why now: Spanish technical framework is crystallizing while test/implementation details are still moving.
Distribution/data advantage: an asesoría already owns the client relationship and knows ERP/accounting stack; repeated workflow across portfolio.
Next falsification: compare with Sage/ERP/Peppol partner tooling and ask whether firms currently maintain this matrix manually in Excel/email.

### 4 — AI Workflow Outcome Ledger for Professional Services
Buyer: law/accounting/advisory firms deploying multiple copilots/agents.
Wedge: workflow-level before/after evidence (cycle time, human touches, exceptions, rework, economic value) independent of model/vendor. Not another AI control plane.
Why now: platforms are commoditizing governance while firms still need to decide which workflows actually produce value.
Distribution hypothesis: implementation/legal-engineering consultancies need defensible ROI evidence across clients.
Next falsification: find existing AI ROI/FinOps products and whether they measure workflow outcomes rather than token/license utilization.

### 5 — Delegated Transaction Proof Reconciler
Buyer: platforms allowing agents to take multi-step commercial/financial actions.
Wedge: reconstruct user intent/authority → agent action chain → order/payment/refund result and flag semantic violations across protocols. Not a payment rail or generic permission tester.
Why now: agentic payment/action ecosystems are crossing into production while responsibility is distributed across layers.
Next falsification: identify whether payment networks/control planes already expose enough end-to-end proof and who owns disputes.

## Selection rationale
This queue intentionally mixes regulation, ecommerce, Spanish advisory operations, professional-services AI economics and agentic commerce. Each candidate has a potential repeatable distribution/workflow advantage rather than merely a novel feature. No build is authorized by this sweep.
