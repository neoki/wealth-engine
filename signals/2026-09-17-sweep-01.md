## Lateral synthesis — five candidates worth one evidence cycle

### A. Agentic Commerce Acceptance Test
Not another checkout integration. Give a merchant/platform a deterministic purchase corpus and test whether multiple agent/payment routes preserve price, SKU, consent, spend limits, refunds and proof-of-intent. Tailwind is strong; potential buyers include ecommerce platforms, PSP integrators and agencies. Main risk: protocols/providers may quickly ship their own conformance suites.

### B. EU App Payment Decision Model
Not another fee calculator. Import real cohort economics and compare IAP vs alternative in-app vs link-out vs alternative distribution including conversion loss, PSP fees, support/refund burden and the 12-month lock. Main risk: episodic market and accessible spreadsheet substitutes. Strong only if agencies/subscription platforms repeat it across portfolios.

### C. Vendor EOL Portfolio Reconciler
Cross-vendor inventory + EOL/change feeds -> affected assets -> replacement path -> owner -> deadline -> evidence. Cisco/Arista/Google/AWS are lateral examples of the same job. Main risk: incumbent ITAM/SAM platforms already own inventory and lifecycle feeds; wedge must be decision/reconciliation or MSP white-label, not alerts.

### D. API/Auth Change-Impact Reconciler
Repository/integration inventory + vendor change -> exact affected integration, auth/scope/config delta, owner and test. HubSpot/Twilio/AWS demonstrate recurring breakage. Main risk: source coverage and access to customer integration inventory.

### E. Agent Permission Regression Harness
Reusable adversarial tests for spend caps, category restrictions, consent, action boundaries and fail-closed behavior across payment/smart-home/enterprise agents. Main risk: too horizontal before protocols stabilize. Potentially high leverage if a platform/integrator repeats tests across many agents.

## Ranking for next evidence cycle
1. Agentic Commerce Acceptance Test — strongest structural tailwind + platform/agency channel + testable without becoming payment processor.
2. API/Auth Change-Impact Reconciler — boring recurring pain with broad vendor surface; investigate competition before building.
3. Vendor EOL Portfolio Reconciler — real recurring pain but likely incumbent-heavy.
4. EU App Payment Decision Model — immediate deadline, but calculator competition and episodic demand.
5. Agent Permission Regression Harness — strategically interesting, currently broad/early.

No candidate is promoted and no public experiment is authorized by this sweep. Next step is one competition/workflow evidence cycle on A and B/D laterally, then rotate.
