# Sweep 06 — 10-round world-driven trial — 2026-09-19

## Purpose
Test the redesigned Wealth Engine: exogenous WORLD inputs, real cognitive diversity, multi-round maturation, and explicit permission to copy/adapt proven profitable businesses.

## Method
Ten independent rounds. Each round sampled a different part of observable reality rather than extending the previous winner. Evidence included public complaints, current pricing, local-market offers, proven services, platform behavior and geographic gaps. Ideas were not required to be software.

## Round log
1. **Unstructured everyday pain** — wedding planning, micro-landlords, scattered WhatsApp/spreadsheets. Signal: people tolerate fragmented workflows until one missed follow-up becomes expensive.
2. **Independent-worker economics** — freelancer scope creep, marketplace sellers, client-service overhead. Signal: real monetary pain, but many direct tools already exist.
3. **Proven boring businesses** — AI receptionist, wedding content creator, pet-waste subscription, senior move management. Key correction: working businesses are opportunity inputs, not disqualifying competition.
4. **Spain localization** — wedding content, vacation-rental management, mystery shopping, local Reels/content. Observable Spanish price bands confirm real spend.
5. **Second-home/concierge world** — recurring Home Watch/property concierge in Spain at roughly €199–€950/month depending on service level. Attractive because it is operational, recurring, local and trust-based.
6. **Geographic whitespace test** — pet-waste removal is a proven ~$89–129/month recurring US service; searches surfaced municipal dog-waste pain in Spain but little obvious equivalent residential subscription supply. Retain as POSSIBLE; geography/housing-density/unit-economics need testing.
7. **Local reputation/Maps** — Spanish services sell review/reputation management from low-cost SaaS to €290+/month managed service. Demand proven but commodity pressure severe. POSSIBLE only with strong distribution/vertical bundling.
8. **Household/senior services** — tech concierge/support, photo digitization, personal assistance. Proven willingness to pay; operational trust/distribution matters more than software novelty.
9. **Sell-it-for-me / recommerce** — Spanish operators already buy, consign or sell goods for customers; examples include clothes, general goods, cars and padel rackets. Interesting primitive: turn household clutter into cash for a commission. Retain POSSIBLE for narrow high-value categories/geographies.
10. **Fast-growing local trend** — Wedding Content Creator in Spain/Galicia. Current evidence shows 2026 Spanish packages commonly hundreds to >€1,000; Galicia press reports increasing direct demand and 2027 bookings. This is a proven, low-capital service with immediate local execution potential. Retain PROMISING, not BRILLIANT yet, because founder/time dependence and acquisition/fulfilment scalability require more evidence.

## Classification after trial

### PROMISING
**Wedding Content Creator production network / brand**
- Why: demand is already paid; low startup capital; Galicia is currently adopting the category; service can be fulfilled by recruited creators rather than Pablo; possible marketplace/agency economics; no software invention required.
- Evidence still needed: Galicia booking density, creator supply economics, CAC/channel (wedding planners/venues/photographers/search), gross margin after creator payout, seasonality.
- Next maturation: price 3 standardized packages and model a network/agency version rather than Pablo personally filming weddings.

**Second-home Home Watch / local concierge**
- Why: proven recurring Spanish memberships (~€199–€950/mo examples), high trust/retention potential, fragmented local supply, operationally simple core (inspection/photos/keys/vendor coordination).
- Evidence still needed: choose geography with sufficient absent-owner density; customer acquisition route; insurance/liability; technician/concierge unit economics.
- Next maturation: compare 3 Spanish geographies and model 25/50/100-home route economics.

### POSSIBLE
**Pet-waste removal subscription adapted to Spain**
- Proven abroad at ~€89–129/month; visible dog-waste pain in Spain; search did not surface obvious equivalent residential category at comparable maturity.
- Risk: Spanish urban housing/garden structure may destroy route density and willingness to pay. Needs geography-first validation.

**Sell-it-for-me vertical**
- Proven commission model in Spain (general consignment, fashion, vehicles, niche sports equipment). Opportunity is not generic Wallapop automation; search for high-value categories where owners hate listing/negotiating/shipping and incumbents are geographically sparse.
- Needs category economics and operational handling model.

**Senior/family tech concierge**
- Spanish/home-market evidence supports €49/month-ish support contracts and international senior-specific concierge demand.
- Could be sold by adult children for parents; trust and support labor may cap margins. Needs channel/economics.

**Vertical AI receptionist reseller/managed service**
- Spanish market is crowded but prices and setups prove spend. Do not build another voice platform. Possible business is distribution + configuration + vertical workflow using commodity infrastructure.
- Needs a vertical/channel where CAC is low and churn low enough; competition alone does not reject.

### REJECT / LOW PRIORITY FOR NOW
- Generic freelancer scope-creep SaaS: pain real but direct products proliferating.
- Generic landlord organizer: many cheap/free incumbents.
- Generic review-management SaaS: strong commoditization and very low software prices.
- Generic wedding-planning spreadsheet: saturated template market.
- Generic menu translation: already heavily commoditized.

## What the trial learned
1. Exogenous world sampling produced much higher domain and business-model diversity than prior sweeps.
2. Wealth Engine had been over-selecting software because web search followed abstract problems; sampling proven services exposes profitable non-software businesses.
3. Competition must be separated into **market proof** and **entry geometry**. Crowded can still be profitable.
4. Geographic copying is a first-class mutation operator: proven elsewhere → test local supply/demand gap.
5. Service businesses can be excellent production candidates when fulfillment can be delegated/networked and acquisition is repeatable.
6. Novelty should not be scored. Expected net capital should.
7. Ten rounds are useful for discovery, but classification remains provisional until each PROMISING candidate receives independent demand, distribution and unit-economics rounds.

## Next action
Do not build yet. Mature the two PROMISING candidates independently:
A) Wedding Content Creator network/brand in Galicia: demand density → acquisition channel → creator supply → unit economics.
B) Second-home Home Watch: geography → route density → acquisition → liability → unit economics.
In parallel, next WORLD sweep must again start from fresh exogenous observations rather than these winners.
