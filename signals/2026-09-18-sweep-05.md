Goal: re-diverge after sweep-04. 40 genuinely fresh signals across regulation, AI-induced operations, mandatory workflows, counterparty friction, reconciliation, productized services, and information asymmetry. Competition is positive demand evidence unless it removes the wedge.

## Signals

1. **Agent approval latency / approval fatigue** — human gates can become the slowest component of agent workflows; opportunity may be measuring and tuning *which* gates deserve humans rather than selling another approval queue.
2. **Agent approval economics** — approval logs contain approval/rejection rates, wait time, reviewer effort and downstream outcomes; potential audit-first service to quantify cost of over-governance.
3. **Agent stale-context-after-wait** — long approval delays can invalidate the state on which an agent proposed an action; potential pre-execution freshness/revalidation primitive.
4. **Agent rubber-stamp detection** — near-100% approval rates plus low dwell time can indicate ceremonial oversight; evidence/audit problem distinct from routing.
5. **Agent reviewer capacity planning** — autonomous throughput can scale faster than human decision capacity; queue forecasting and staffing may become an operations category.
6. **Agent approval packet quality** — missing context forces reviewers to reconstruct cases across systems; potential packet-completeness scoring.
7. **Agent exception compression** — cluster repeated approvals and convert safe recurring classes into policy, shrinking human workload over time.
8. **Agent autonomy budget** — allocate limited human attention by economic/risk consequence rather than static tool-level allowlists.

## Ranked candidates

Scoring lens: pain × WTP × reachable buyer × cheap falsifiability × differentiation × automation leverage.

1. **Agent Approval Economics Auditor** — ingest approval/event logs (CSV/JSON first), quantify human wait cost, rubber-stamp patterns, unnecessary gates, stale-context risk, and candidate gates for policy/post-action review. Cheap falsification; no runtime integration required initially.
2. **Customer Contract AI-Use Restriction Mapper** — contract clauses/engagement rules → allowed/prohibited AI workflows → exceptions. Strong regulatory/professional-services adjacency; must falsify against AI governance/GRC incumbents.
3. **Security Questionnaire Promise-to-Reality Auditor** — outbound diligence answers/RFP promises → current operational evidence → stale/contradictory representations. Service-first wedge; connects sales promises to runtime reality.
4. **Field-Service Warranty Reimbursement Leakage Auditor** — completed work → warranty eligibility → evidence → unsubmitted/denied claim → recoverable cash. Different vertical from prior grant/rebate work.
5. **Agent Stale-Context Revalidation Gate** — when approval delay exceeds threshold or upstream state changes, re-read critical state before execution. Potential infrastructure primitive; likely feature rather than standalone company unless pain proves broad.

## Immediate falsification target

`agent-approval-economics-auditor`

Do **not** build another approval queue. Test whether existing HITL/governance platforms already turn historical approval logs into recommendations that reduce human review while preserving control, including: approval-rate/dwell-time analysis, rubber-stamp detection, economic cost of waiting, gate-removal candidates, policy threshold tuning, and before/after autonomy metrics.

- Continuous AI-vendor change monitoring is already being commercialized (Changecast and adjacent vendor-change products), so generic vendor monitoring is not promoted.

## Decision
