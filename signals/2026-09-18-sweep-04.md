## Fresh evidence sampled

- Revenue recovery is becoming a product category rather than merely AR: RevExOS sells a $12k–$24k/year revenue-leak recovery layer for multi-client accounting/professional-services firms; RevCore detects closed deals not invoiced, mismatches, missed renewals and failed payments; Trullion now markets contract/billing/financial-data revenue-leakage detection. This validates willingness to pay but weakens a generic horizontal leakage product.
- Contract follow-through is also productizing: ObliSpace and NoticeCue extract renewal/notice obligations; Escalake specifically turns index-linked escalation clauses into versioned calculations with buyer/supplier sign-off. Generic deadline/index calculation is therefore not enough. The more interesting wedge is **capture**: prove that a due contractual increase actually reached the operational price/invoice, and audit historical misses.
- Supplier portals overwhelmingly solve the buyer's side (vendor self-service, invoice submission/status). RiditStack, Infilabs, ProcurePulse, Traild and TenvioCloud all reduce buyer AP status traffic. This leaves a plausible asymmetric question worth falsifying: how a supplier with many large customers reconciles statuses/rejections across many *different customer-owned portals*.
- Warranty recovery has strong economics but vertical entrants already exist: ClaimHound claims HVAC contractors miss most eligible claims and charges only on recovery; Alpine and CoreRMA automate warranty eligibility/recovery. Generic warranty recovery is not a whitespace market.
- Construction cash recovery is explicit: OwePath tracks applications, variations, retention and overdue amounts. Any construction candidate must be narrower than a commercial-control dashboard.
- Freight audit/D&D is highly monetizable but active: AuditDray, FreightAudit Pilot, Mirage Metrics, ZingTMS and DemurrageIQ already check invoices/contracts/free-time/evidence and automate disputes. Do not enter generically.
- Permit/COI expiration tracking is rapidly commoditizing (PermitTracker, PermitsAlert, Flow365 Expiry, Checkmate, CoverChecks, CoverSynx, COISoftware). A useful wedge must connect expiry to a revenue-producing operation that becomes impossible, not reminders.
- Grant compliance is active (AwardTrace, GrantGuard, GrantableHQ, GrantSteward), including deadlines, evidence, reimbursement and spending rules. Generic grant compliance is crowded.
- RFP compliance matrices and security questionnaires are exceptionally crowded in 2026 (Projectory, ProposalMatrix, Bidara, RFPAI, BeginRFP, Orbiq, Compyl, Tribble, SecurityPal). Avoid generic document-to-matrix generation.
- Appointment no-show/deposit recovery is also commoditizing quickly (ShowUp, HoldSlot, TimeSlot, ReservaSegura, PreventNoShows). Distribution is easy but differentiation is poor.

## Ranked queue for falsification

1. **Contract Indexation Capture Auditor** — not escalation calculation. Reconcile signed indexation/escalation clauses against actual price masters, invoices and renewals; find increases that were contractually available but never operationalized, quantify recoverable/forward revenue, and produce the action/evidence. Start service-first with historical audit because ROI can be demonstrated before software. Kill if CLM/revenue-leakage products already do clause→official index→actual billed price→missed increase recovery for ordinary B2B service contracts.
2. **Supplier-Side Multi-Portal Receivables Reconciler** — buyer-side vendor portals are abundant; test the inverse pain for suppliers serving enterprises: many customer portals, each with invoice status, rejection reason, PO mismatch and payment date. Kill if supplier AR tools already aggregate heterogeneous buyer-owned portals without buyer cooperation.
3. **Construction Variation-to-Cash Gap Auditor** — approved/communicated variation or price escalation exists but never reaches payment application/invoice/retention position. Narrower than construction ERP. Kill if OwePath/Payapps/Autodesk-style tools automatically reconcile source change evidence to actual billed/certified cash across systems.
4. **Operational Credential Revenue Blocker** — certification/license/permit/insurance prerequisite expires and makes a scheduled person/site/job non-deliverable. Rank by booked revenue/margin at risk, not compliance severity. This intentionally tests the recurring constraint→production primitive in a fourth market.
5. **Grant Reimbursement Leakage Auditor** — identify eligible incurred spend that has not been converted into a reimbursement request because evidence, coding or filing is incomplete. Monetization could be audit/recovery share rather than SaaS. Kill if post-award grant tools already reconcile ledger spend→award eligibility→evidence→unclaimed reimbursement at transaction level for the target segment.

## Lateral connections

- **Revenue capture before optimization:** #1/#2/#6/#7/#8/#9/#17/#18/#20/#24/#31 all concern value already contractually or operationally earned but not converted to cash. This is potentially a stronger discovery axis than generic automation because ROI is directly measurable and success-fee/service-first monetization can reduce buyer friction.
- **Asymmetric workflow opportunity:** enterprise buyers increasingly impose portals and processes on suppliers. Software tends to optimize the portal owner; the counterparty may be forced to operate across many incompatible systems. Search explicitly for these 'many counterparties, many portals, one small team' geometries.
- **Constraint→production primitive survives but is not privileged:** #26/#29/#30 reproduce it independently. Keep observing without forcing future sweeps into it.

## Engine decision

Do not build yet. Falsify **Contract Indexation Capture Auditor** first. Positive threshold before public experiment: (a) evidence that missed/late indexation creates material recoverable or forward revenue in at least 3 ordinary B2B service/industrial contexts, (b) no incumbent already closes the full clause→index→actual billing→recovery loop for the target segment, and (c) a credible low-friction data path using contracts + invoice/price exports. If it survives, prefer a historical audit/service-first experiment before a public SaaS; any later web belongs under a descriptive rockrai.com subdomain and preferably rockrai-experiment-factory.
