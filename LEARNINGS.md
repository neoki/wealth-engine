# Wealth Engine — Durable Learnings

Principles earned from evidence/failure; not a diary.

## Discovery
- Breadth is a feature. Repeatedly mining one regulatory/B2B pattern creates correlated bets.
- Exogenous entropy matters: capture heterogeneous observations from the world before applying accumulated engine patterns.
- Separate noisy WORLD memory from compressed ENGINE memory so past learnings do not filter what can be noticed.
- Strong signals trigger lateral exploration, mutation and recombination, not only vertical research.
- Originality is not a goal. Proven profitable models are first-class opportunity sources.

## Maturation and validation
- Ideas should mature across multiple materially different evidence rounds before terminal classification; default minimum is 3 unless decisive falsification exists.
- Classify uncertainty instead of forcing binary promote/reject: POSSIBLE, PROMISING and BRILLIANT preserve valuable optionality.
- Traffic/bots/crawlers/self-visits are not traction. Portfolio sharing, pilots, willingness to pay and payments are stronger evidence.
- When the next uncertainty requires buyer evidence, retain/classify and rotate; do not substitute endless research/features.
- Competition often validates demand. A market can support another actor without novelty. Test market room, acquisition, economics and execution.
- Copy/adapt working businesses when geography, language, segment, channel, UX, automation, price, bundling, speed, brand or sheer market size leaves profitable room.
- Regulation: announcement/proposal is a signal; published operative text validates. Reconcile exact obligation, scope, dates and transitions before building.

## Building
- Evidence before code; code before infrastructure; irreversible spend last.
- BRILLIANT opportunities can graduate to PRODUCTION; production is an independent child project.
- Do not build another scanner when mature scanners/exports exist unless there is demonstrable room for another profitable actor.

## Portfolio
- Bound active exploration; profitable businesses are not disposable experiments.
- Avoid correlated portfolios.
- Optimize net capital and reproducible acquisition, not novelty, repositories, features or websites.
- A simple clone with distribution can dominate an ingenious product without customers.

## Memory and meta-learning
- Git is canonical; chat is cache.
- Persist compressed state/decisions, not transcripts.
- Every important rule must survive a fresh-agent session.
- Compression alone is insufficient for meta-learning: preserve structured trajectories of decisions, evidence, costs and outcomes so search policy can be replayed and improved.
- Failed/dead branches are useful data for learning stopping and branching policies; do not erase them from trajectory memory.
- Separate object-level learning (which opportunities are good) from policy-level learning (which exploration strategy finds good opportunities efficiently).
