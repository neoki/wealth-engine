# Wealth Engine — Durable Learnings

Principles earned from evidence/failure; not a diary.

## Discovery
- Breadth is a feature. Repeatedly mining one regulatory/B2B pattern creates correlated bets.
- Sweep heterogeneous spaces: B2B operations, consumer, apps/games, creator, marketplaces, AI productivity, data, ecommerce, agent economy and event-driven opportunities.
- Strong signals trigger lateral exploration, not only vertical research.

## Validation
- Traffic/bots/crawlers/self-visits are not traction. Portfolio sharing, pilots, willingness to pay and payments are stronger evidence.
- When the next uncertainty requires buyer evidence, mark `buyer-blocked` and rotate; do not substitute more research/features.
- Competition often validates demand. Test differentiation, underserved segment/workflow/geography, automation, price and distribution before rejecting.
- Regulation: announcement/proposal is a signal; published operative text validates. Reconcile exact obligation, scope, dates and transitions before building.

## Building
- Do not build another scanner when mature scanners/exports exist. Look for missing decision, reconciliation, QA, acceptance-test or workflow layers.
- Evidence before code; code before infrastructure; irreversible spend last.
- Promoted opportunities leave Wealth Engine. Child projects converge; Wealth Engine diverges.

## Portfolio
- Bound active exploration; profitable businesses are not disposable experiments.
- Avoid correlated portfolios: many regulatory micro-SaaS ideas are still one underlying bet.
- Optimize net capital and reproducible acquisition, not repositories/features/websites.

## Memory
- Git is canonical; chat is cache.
- Persist compressed state/decisions, not transcripts.
- Every important rule must survive a fresh-agent session.
