# Spain B2B e-invoice timing signal — 2026-09-14

## Signal
Royal Decree 238/2026 has established the operating framework for mandatory B2B electronic invoicing in Spain. The BOE states that publication of the implementing ministerial order starts the statutory adoption clock. Current specialist reporting expects that order to enter into force on 1 October 2026, with mandatory application from 1 October 2027 for businesses above €8M turnover and 1 October 2028 for the remaining businesses/professionals.

## Why this changes the experiment
This is no longer a generic future-compliance proposition. The market is entering a concrete preparation window in which companies and advisory firms need to distinguish three separate questions: B2B electronic invoicing, VeriFactu requirements, and whether existing accounting/invoicing software actually covers each obligation.

A September 2026 tax-advisory report also highlights current confusion: firms that merely book invoices for autónomos are not necessarily required to adapt that accounting software to VeriFactu, while software used to issue invoices remains in scope. This distinction creates a practical diagnostic wedge for advisory firms managing many client configurations.

## Wealth Engine decision
Do not create another e-invoicing product. Strengthen the existing `spain-einvoice-readiness` validation offer and test demand before building software.

### Target buyer
Spanish advisory/accounting firms and SMEs that need a fast answer to: "What must change in our current invoicing stack, for which clients/entities, and by when?"

### Economic test
Keep the existing €79 one-time readiness review as the initial purchase signal. Manual fulfilment is acceptable until demand is demonstrated.

### Kill gate
Kill or materially reshape the offer if direct outreach/organic traffic produces no qualified requests after a reasonable distribution test, or if incumbent ERP/accounting vendors make the diagnostic redundant at zero marginal cost.
