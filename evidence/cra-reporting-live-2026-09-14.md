# Signal: CRA Article 14 reporting is now live — 2026-09-14

## Fresh event
The EU Cyber Resilience Act Article 14 reporting obligations became applicable on 11 September 2026. ENISA launched the CRA Single Reporting Platform (SRP) the same day.

Manufacturers of products with digital elements made available in the EU must report actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents affecting product security. The operational clock is unusually short: early warning within 24 hours, fuller notification within 72 hours, then a final report (14 days after a corrective/mitigating measure is available for an actively exploited vulnerability; one month after the 72-hour notification for a severe incident).

Official sources checked 2026-09-14:
- European Commission CRA reporting: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-reporting
- ENISA SRP launch: https://www.enisa.europa.eu/news/the-cra-single-reporting-platform-is-launched
- ENISA SRP FAQ: https://www.enisa.europa.eu/topics/product-security/single-reporting-platform-srp/frequently-asked-questions

## Buyer pain
This is not a future compliance deadline: the reporting duty is already live. A manufacturer cannot improvise ownership, awareness timestamping, triage, escalation, user notification and SRP filing after an exploit is discovered and still reliably hit 24 hours.

Potential wedge: a fixed-price **CRA 24h Reporting Drill** for small software/device manufacturers selling into the EU. Deliverable: scope sanity-check + named internal roles + awareness/triage decision tree + 24h/72h/final-report checklist + tabletop drill + evidence pack. This is narrower and more urgent than a generic CRA compliance audit.

## Competition check
Do NOT build a broad CRA compliance SaaS. The market already has several products/tools (CRAcheck, Normproof, cyberresilienceact.eu, Seentrix, Cramio and others) covering scope, SBOM, documentation and ongoing compliance. Free scope checkers are abundant.

The possible gap is operational readiness for the newly-live reporting workflow, especially a human-assisted one-off drill rather than another dashboard.
