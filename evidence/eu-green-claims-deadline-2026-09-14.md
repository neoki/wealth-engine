# Signal: EU green-claims cleanup deadline — 2026-09-14

## Trigger
Directive (EU) 2024/825 (Empowering Consumers for the Green Transition) is due to apply from 27 September 2026. Current legal explainers describe no general transition relief for consumer-facing environmental claims on existing products/communications. Generic claims, unsupported sustainability labels, whole-product claims based on narrow attributes, and certain offset-based climate-neutral claims are among the targeted practices.

## Market pain
This creates an unusually concrete remediation job for B2C companies: find environmental language and sustainability badges across product pages, website copy, packaging/creative inventory and marketing material, classify risky claims, attach evidence, and produce a rewrite/removal queue before the deadline.

## Potential wedge
**Green Claims Sweep** — fixed-price, one-off pre-deadline audit rather than another broad compliance SaaS.

Input: website/domain plus optional product-page export.
Output: inventory of detected environmental claims, risk category, exact URL/location, evidence-needed field, recommended action (keep/substantiate/rewrite/remove), and a prioritized remediation list.

Test price: EUR 99–199 depending on page count. Start manually/agent-assisted; do not build crawling infrastructure until demand exists.

## Why this may be stronger than generic compliance software
- Deadline is 13 days away at signal capture.
- The job is bounded and legible to marketing/e-commerce buyers.
- Value can be demonstrated on the customer's existing public website before asking for deep system access.
- Delivery can initially be service-like, minimizing engineering cost.

## Important caveat: Spain
Spain was among Member States reported in 2026 as not having communicated complete transposition by the March deadline. Do not claim that the EU directive itself automatically creates an identical directly enforceable Spanish prohibition on 27 September without verifying Spain's national transposition status. Initial commercial targeting should therefore prioritize jurisdictions with confirmed transposition or frame Spain as readiness/risk review pending national implementation.

## Experiment gate
Do **not** create a public web experiment yet. First validate competition and find evidence of buyer urgency. Promote to an experiment only if there is a credible acquisition path and the offer is not already commoditized by free scanners.

## Sources checked
- Latham & Watkins, 9 Sep 2026: new rules apply from 27 Sep 2026; B2C scope; existing products/claims affected; no general transitional relief described.
- European Outdoor Group summary, May 2026: Commission infringement procedures against 20 Member States including Spain for incomplete transposition at that point.
- Spanish European Consumer Centre / Ministry consumer information on Directive 2024/825 and Spain's Sustainable Consumption Law process.

## Status
RESEARCH — promising time-boxed service wedge, not yet an experiment.
