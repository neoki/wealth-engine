# Wealth Engine — Cognitive Society

Status: design contract for the next exploration cycle.

## Why

A single orchestrator repeatedly generating, ranking and refining its own ideas tends to collapse onto a narrow semantic neighborhood. Wealth Engine must preserve independent world-models long enough for genuinely different opportunity spaces to emerge and collide.

This is not a fixed business playbook. It is an adaptive ecology for generating and testing routes to net capital.

## Core rule

**Independence before conversation; conversation before allocation; reality before confidence.**

No explorer sees another explorer's proposals during its first pass. The allocator does not seed explorers with the current favorite thesis. Existing experiments are evidence, not the boundaries of the search space.

## Initial independent explorers

These are deliberately heterogeneous starting populations, not permanent roles. They may split, mutate, merge or disappear based on useful discoveries.

### Wild Consumer
Explores mass-consumer behavior: apps, games, entertainment, status, identity, curiosity, competition, habit, sharing, novelty and other reasons people voluntarily spend attention or money. It must not assume SaaS or B2B.

### Money Hunter
Starts from observable flows of money rather than technologies or industries. Looks for spreads, commissions, expensive intermediaries, recurring purchases, underpriced information, resale, lead value, marketplaces and other places where value can be intercepted or improved.

### Frontier Hacker
Starts from newly cheap or newly possible capabilities: models, APIs, automation, devices, protocols and platform changes. Its question is not "what AI SaaS can we build?" but "what became possible recently that changes an economic constraint?"

### Boring Capitalist
Prefers simple, legible economics: existing demand, clear buyer, ugly/manual markets, brokerage, services, licensing, resale and operational improvements. It is hostile to technological elegance without a route to cash.

### Alien Explorer
Receives the least project context. Its purpose is to search outside the semantic neighborhood created by Wealth Engine's history. It should deliberately surface categories absent from recent cycles and may propose strange hypotheses when they are cheap to falsify.

## Agora

After independent exploration, proposals enter an adversarial conversation. Explorers may attack, combine, mutate or invest fictitious capital in other proposals. The goal is not consensus. Productive disagreement and unexpected recombination are first-class outputs.

Every proposal keeps provenance: originating explorer, assumptions, critiques, mutations and supporters. A proposal that survives only because all agents share the same assumption should be penalized.

## Allocator

The allocator does not choose the most eloquent idea. It allocates small experimental budgets based on expected information value and asymmetric economic upside while preserving portfolio diversity.

There is no mandatory sequence such as buyer → pain → offer → distribution. An opportunity may originate in demand, technology, culture, distribution, regulation, behavior, data, price discontinuity or another signal. Before significant capital is committed, however, the experiment must expose a falsifiable path to real-world economic evidence.

Profitable experiments leave the experimental slot pool and become persistent assets. Experimental concurrency limits must never delete a proven winner.

## Reputation and internal capital

Explorers receive fictitious capital and may back proposals after the Agora phase. Real outcomes update reputation by domain and type of prediction. The system should learn not merely which ideas worked, but which explorers are calibrated in which environments.

Reputation must decay and remain domain-specific to prevent one historical success from creating permanent authority.

## Anti-collapse constraints

- Do not give every explorer the same context with a different persona label.
- Do not expose the current leading opportunity before independent generation.
- Measure semantic/category diversity across successive candidate pools.
- Reserve exploration budget for proposals unlike recent experiments.
- Periodically create context-light explorers to escape accumulated assumptions.
- Preserve minority proposals when falsification is cheap and upside is asymmetric.
- Treat traffic, crawler activity and technical success as evidence only when they bear on the economic hypothesis being tested.
- Net capital generated is the terminal economic measure; intermediate metrics are diagnostic, never substitutes for it.

## First session contract

The first society session should produce independent candidate sets before any new product is deployed. It should then record:

1. proposals from each explorer without cross-contamination;
2. critiques and mutations created by the Agora;
3. fictitious capital allocations and reasons;
4. a small set of experiments selected for real-world falsification;
5. explicit evidence that would cause each experiment to be killed, persisted or scaled.

No explorer is required to produce a SaaS, MCP, invoice, SME or AI-related idea. Category surprise is desirable when economically coherent.

## Evolution

This document defines initial selection pressures, not a permanent organization chart. Agent populations, prompts, memories, debate topology, allocation rules and experiment types should evolve from observed performance. The architecture fails if it merely replaces one rigid pipeline with six rigid personas.
